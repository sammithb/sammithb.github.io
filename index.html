About

I am currently a senior studying Mathematics at Purdue University. Broadly, I am interested in applied mathematics, particularly numerical optimization, dynamical systems, and applied probability. More recently, I have also become interested in the mathematics of imaging science and optimal control, especially problems that connect mathematical theory with computation and real-world applications.

Outside of mathematics, I am an avid sports fan, particularly of cricket, football, and basketball. I also enjoy running, hiking, and traveling whenever I get the chance. More about these interests, along with other fun parts of my life, can be found on the Personal section of this website.

Projects & Research

Exact Algorithms and Solvers for Total Variation Compressed Sensing

with Prof. Xiangxiong Zhang
Studying exact optimization methods for total variation compressed sensing and developing numerical solvers for one- and two-dimensional problems.

Instructional Development for Linear Programming and Optimization

with Prof. Aaron Yip
Developing instructional and computational materials for Purdue’s MA 421 course, with an emphasis on making optimization concepts and algorithms more accessible through examples, computational demonstrations, and application-based exercises.

Characterizing Visibility Graphs of Polygons

with Prof. Shuyi Weng
Studying combinatorial and algorithmic questions related to visibility graphs of polygons and tetrahedralizable polyhedra, and developing an interactive Visibility Graph Visualizer for computational exploration.
